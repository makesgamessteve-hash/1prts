# 08 · Setting & Factions

## 1. The setting

**Veyra** is a land of river valleys, dark pine forests, chalk downs and a stormy coast. Above it,
when the clouds part, you can see the silhouettes of **floating citadels** drifting in the high
sky.

Six centuries ago, the **High Imperium** ruled Veyra from those citadels. Its sky-mages bound
great **aetherstones** to lift whole fortresses into the air, and for three hundred years no
power on the ground could touch them. Then came the **Skyfall**. Nobody agrees on why, but in a
single night the aetherstones failed, and most of the citadels fell. The craters are still
there, now lakes ringed with ruins. A handful of citadels stayed aloft, dark and silent,
drifting on the high winds ever since.

Not everyone lived in the Imperium's shadow. Deep below the chalk downs, the **Durrow** had
built their courts in the dark long before the first citadel rose, and when the citadels fell they
sealed their gates and waited. Out on the islands, the Imperium's old harbor towns and the
amphibious **Hesk** of the reefs swore a pact of free cities that answers to no throne.

Today four peoples fight over Veyra's gold, its forests, its stone and its seas, and over the
chance to wake a citadel and rule the sky again.

### Tone
Grounded high fantasy with mud on its boots. Wooden palisades and stone keeps, torchlight,
banners in the rain, the creak of a galleon's timbers. Magic is real but rare and costly. War is
seen through the eyes of the person who has to send others into it. Soldiers have names, and
losses are counted. No side is purely good or evil.

### The look of magic
Magic shows up as **glowing lines of light**: the war table's illusion map, the shimmer of a
marking beam, runes on a ward, the threads of the stone network. This fits the retro art style
(line-drawn holograms in the renderer) and makes all magical displays look like they belong to
the same world.

## 2. Factions

| Faction | Peoples | Home ground | Commander title |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Crown of Esterholt | Humans, with dwarven allies | The field: roads, walls and castles | Lord or Lady Marshal |
| The Wildborn Clans | Ghorr, Kobb, ridge giants | Forests, hills and the sky | Huntlord |
| The Gloam Courts | Durrow | Tunnels and the deep caverns | High Delver |
| The Brine League | Island humans and Hesk | The sea, on and under the water | Tide-Admiral |

### The Crown of Esterholt
A human kingdom of walled towns and river ports, allied with the dwarves of the **Ironhollow
Holds**, who trade powder and engineering for gold.

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| Doctrine | Fortify, hold, then strike with heavy cavalry and siege; win by organization and firepower |
| Strengths | Stone keeps and walls, knights, crossbows, dwarven bombards, war galleons, skyships |
| Weaknesses | Slow to move; needs farms and roads; expensive units |
| Signature units | Lantern Knights, siege tower, war galleon, dwarven skyship, sky galleon |
| Workers | Serfs |
| Visual language | Whitewashed stone, blue and gold heraldry, steel plate, timber-framed houses, golden magic lines |
| Voice | Formal and loyal ("Aye, my lord." "By your command, Marshal.") |
| Commander title | Lord or Lady Marshal |

### The Wildborn Clans
A confederation of hill and forest clans: the big, tusked **Ghorr**, the small, clever **Kobb**,
and the giants of the ridges who fight beside them. They follow the herds and the seasons, and
they remember the High Imperium as conquerors.

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| Doctrine | Move, raid, burn; win by speed, numbers and striking the enemy's gatherers and farms |
| Strengths | Mobile war camp on the behemoth, boar riders, giants, fire, shamans, wyverns, rocs and drakes |
| Weaknesses | Timber palisades that burn; fewer ranged units; little artillery apart from the giants |
| Signature units | Behemoth, ridge giant, bonehulk, serpent rider, elder drake |
| Workers | Kobb |
| Visual language | Hide, bone and carved wood, ochre and moss-green paint, antler totems, green spirit-fire magic lines |
| Voice | Rough and proud ("Hrah! We go." "The Huntlord speaks!") |
| Commander title | Huntlord |

### The Gloam Courts
The underground realm of the **Durrow**: short, broad, grey-skinned people with large dark eyes
that see well in dim light and badly in bright sun. They farm fungus, cut stone, grow crystal and
tame the beasts of the deep. Their courts are ruled by councils of elders who seldom see the sky.

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| Doctrine | Strike from below; undermine, ambush, deny light; win by surprise and by fortresses nobody can reach |
| Strengths | Tunnels and undermining, night fighting, headquarters deep underground, heavy infantry, crystal magic |
| Weaknesses | Weaker in daylight and in open fields; slow on the surface; a small navy that sticks to rivers and lakes; few flyers |
| Signature units | Tunnel wyrm, olm brute, glow-drill, lantern moth, the Sunken Throne |
| Workers | Delvers |
| Visual language | Dark basalt, carved pillars, copper filigree, moth-wing banners, blue-violet glowstone magic lines |
| Voice | Quiet and ceremonial ("Below, and onward." "As the stone wills, High Delver.") |
| Commander title | High Delver |

### The Brine League
A league of free island cities. Their human sailors and the amphibious **Hesk** of the reefs (tall,
grey-green, gilled and web-handed people who are as at home under the water as above it) share
one fleet and one purse.

| Aspect | Description |
|---|---|
| Doctrine | Rule the sea; raid the coasts; trade; strike from under the water; bend the weather |
| Strengths | The strongest navy, underwater units, amphibious landings, storm magic, trade at market towns |
| Weaknesses | Weak far from water; little heavy cavalry; light buildings; needs coasts, rivers and harbors |
| Signature units | Tidehold raft-city, war carrack, wind-barque, manta riders, diving bell |
| Workers | Dockhands (on land) and Hesk pearl divers (under water) |
| Visual language | Whitewashed coral stone, sailcloth, rope and brass, shell inlay, turquoise magic lines |
| Voice | Brash sea talk ("Aye aye, Admiral! Hoist and away!" "Deep and quiet, Admiral.") |
| Commander title | Tide-Admiral |

All four factions fill the same classic roles (worker, melee, ranged, cavalry, caster, siege,
saboteur, flyer, transport, warship), so balance and AI stay manageable. Asymmetry comes from
signature units, home grounds, building styles (stone, timber and fire, carved rock, coral and
sailcloth) and their command posts (the fixed keep, the walking behemoth, the court deep
underground, the floating raft-city).

### The Custodians (later milestone)
The ancient guardians of the floating citadels: constructs of stone and aether, built by the
High Imperium. Waking a citadel wakes them too. They have no commander, no crews and no
interiors fit for people.
- Built from **voxels**, so they look clearly different from the low-poly people and beasts of
  Veyra.
- They "repair" the land the way the Imperium wanted it: they rebuild ruins, reclaim aether
  wells and drive every side away from the citadels.
- They give the campaign its last act and give skirmish an optional neutral or hostile side.
