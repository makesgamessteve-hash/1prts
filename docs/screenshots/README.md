# Screenshots

`01`–`12`, `18`–`22` (Harrowmark: the outer ward, the wall-walk, the view from the Raven
Tower, the inner ward and the King's Gate) and `26`–`27` are taken with `tests/game/scenarios/tour.lua` at 960×540 (OpenGL 3.3 core on Mesa
llvmpipe under Xvfb, retro preset 270 lines):

```sh
xvfb-run -a ./build/dev/bin/1stprts --size 960x540 --scenario tests/game/scenarios/tour.lua --out docs/screenshots
```

`13`–`17` show the user interface at 1280×720: the front-end menu, the video options, the
Credits screen, the touch controls (phone browsers) and the developer console with the
labeled observer camera (developer builds only).

`23`–`25` show the story told in first person: the prologue's subtitles on the road to the
King's Gate, talking to a gate guard, and the castellan in the great hall with his orders on
the notice board.

`26` and `27` show what the fun audit (`tools/fun/README.md`) led to: a title card the first
time the Marshal walks into a part of the castle, and one of the castellan's orders done (the
arrows counted in the barracks), reported by the quartermaster's tally.

`70`–`75` show Skyfall (M6) on the Caldmere: Caldrith rising, the skyship climbing into the
storm layer, the view above the clouds, the throne hall's war table over its scrying floor, the
weightless aether core with Ashby at the stone, and elder drakes over the courtyard. They are
staged scenes taken at 960×540 under Xvfb like the others.

`80`–`85` show Hollow Roads (M7) on Underhill: the tunnel gate at the foot of Merewatch Hill, a
gallery lit by glowstone, Caldrith's broken chain in the glowstone cavern, the Hollow Court's war
room under the ground, the Court's guard at its gatehouse, and a tunnel wyrm burst up out of the
field.

`90`–`95` show Deep Water (M8) at Reefholm: the League's Tidehold beyond the reef, its council
deck, a pearl bed under the water (with the breath bar), the sunken sky-barge, the diving bell's
shell table on the seabed, and the market town of Saltwick.

`96`–`99` show Every Screen (M9): the war table in the deuteranopia palette and in high contrast
(rings mark your own companies, squares the enemy's), the touch buttons laid out left-handed, and
the Access tab of the options menu.
