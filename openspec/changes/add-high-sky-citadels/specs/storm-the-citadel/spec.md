# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The *Storm the Citadel* scenario (story act V) and the citadel phase in skirmishes.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Storm the Citadel
The Caldmere map SHALL raise Caldrith out of the lake; the Crown SHALL win by waking it and holding it clear of Wildborn boarders, and SHALL lose if the boarders break its aetherstone.

#### Scenario: Storming Caldrith
- **WHEN** the commander flies up through the storm with the skyship's company and Magister, docks, clears the boarders and wakes Caldrith with Ashby
- **THEN** the match ends in the Crown's victory

#### Scenario: Caldrith broken
- **WHEN** the Wildborn boarders hold Caldrith's core alone for long enough
- **THEN** Caldrith is broken and the Crown loses

### Requirement: A citadel phase in skirmishes
The Thornmere skirmish map SHALL have a dormant citadel drifting on a track over the field.

#### Scenario: A citadel over Thornmere
- **WHEN** a skirmish on Thornmere starts
- **THEN** a dormant citadel drifts on its track high over the field
