# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The *Under the Hill* scenario (story act VI).

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Under the Hill
The Underhill map SHALL put the Crown on Merewatch Hill over the Gloam's caverns, with a deep shaft down to them; the Crown SHALL win when the Gloam's headquarters falls and lose if its keep falls.

#### Scenario: Taking the Hollow Court
- **WHEN** the Crown's companies go down through the tunnel road and destroy the Gloam's headquarters
- **THEN** the match ends in the Crown's victory

#### Scenario: Caldrith's chain
- **WHEN** the commander rides the deep shaft down into the cavern under the hill
- **THEN** they find Caldrith's broken chain running down into the glowstone cavern
