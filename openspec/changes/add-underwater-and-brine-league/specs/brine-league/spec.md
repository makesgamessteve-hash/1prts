# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The Brine League as the fourth faction: pearls, its units, ships and buildings, the Tidehold, the wind-barque and four-faction matches.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Pearls
The League's pearl divers SHALL gather pearls from pearl beds and bring them to a pearl house or headquarters.

#### Scenario: Pearl divers at work
- **WHEN** pearl divers are sent to a pearl bed
- **THEN** the League's pearls grow

### Requirement: The League's soldiers and ships
The League SHALL train its units and build its ships: harpooneers SHALL pin what they strike, hull-borers SHALL strike ships off the shore, and its ships SHALL be built at its yards.

#### Scenario: Harpoons and hull-borers
- **WHEN** harpooneers strike a rider and hull-borers reach an enemy ship lying off the shore
- **THEN** the rider is slowed for a while and the ship takes heavy damage

#### Scenario: A brigantine from the yard
- **WHEN** a shipwright yard on the shore is ordered to build a brigantine
- **THEN** a brigantine is launched into the water

### Requirement: The Tidehold
The Tidehold SHALL be the League's headquarters that sails, carrying whoever stands on it, with a war table on its council deck; it SHALL sink when destroyed.

#### Scenario: The raft-city sails
- **WHEN** the Tidehold is sent across the water with the commander on its council deck
- **THEN** it sails there with them aboard, and its war table shows the war

### Requirement: The wind-barque
The wind-barque SHALL fly faster inside the storm layer's winds.

#### Scenario: Riding the storm winds
- **WHEN** a wind-barque flies the same distance inside and below the storm band
- **THEN** it arrives sooner inside the band

### Requirement: Four factions
A skirmish on Tidesend SHALL be played by the Crown, the Wildborn, the Gloam and the League.

#### Scenario: Four peoples
- **WHEN** a skirmish on Tidesend runs for a few minutes
- **THEN** every side of the four factions has built and trained
