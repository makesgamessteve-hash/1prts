# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The League's casters: the Weatherwright and the Tidecantor.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Weatherwright
The Weatherwright SHALL cast Squall (puts out fires, brings down flyers and pushes ships along a lane), Lightning Lash (strikes a target and jumps to two more) and Fog Bank (cuts sight in an area).

#### Scenario: Weatherwright spells
- **WHEN** a Weatherwright casts Squall over a burning building and a wyvern, Lightning Lash at a company, and Fog Bank over its own ships
- **THEN** the fire goes out and the wyvern is hurt and brought low, three men are struck, and the ships cannot be seen from beyond the fog's edge

### Requirement: The Tidecantor
The Tidecantor SHALL cast Tide Surge (knocks troops down), Brine Mend (heals a company, more near water) and Undertow (slows ships).

#### Scenario: Tidecantor spells
- **WHEN** a Tidecantor casts Tide Surge on an enemy company, Brine Mend on wounded marines by the shore and Undertow on an enemy ship
- **THEN** the company is knocked down, the marines heal, and the ship sails at half speed
