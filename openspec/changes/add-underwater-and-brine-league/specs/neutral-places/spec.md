# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Neutral market towns and lighthouses.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Market towns
A market town SHALL buy and sell resources for gold at prices that move with trade, better for the League, and the side holding it SHALL get folk and rumours of enemies nearby.

#### Scenario: Trading at the market
- **WHEN** a side sells timber at a market town twice and then holds the town
- **THEN** the second sale fetches less than the first, and the side gains food and old sightings of enemies near the town

### Requirement: Lighthouses
A held lighthouse SHALL see far over the sea for its holder and carry its sending stones.

#### Scenario: Holding the lighthouse
- **WHEN** a side's company holds a lighthouse
- **THEN** the side sees ships far out to sea from it, and companies near it hear the stones
