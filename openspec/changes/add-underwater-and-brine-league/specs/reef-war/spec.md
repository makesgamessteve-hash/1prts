# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The *Reef War* scenario (story act VII).

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Reef War
The Reefholm map SHALL set the Crown's harbor against the League's Tidehold among the reefs; the Crown SHALL win when the Tidehold sinks and lose if its keep falls.

#### Scenario: Sinking the Tidehold
- **WHEN** the Crown's galleon and caravel bombard the Tidehold until it sinks
- **THEN** the match ends in the Crown's victory

#### Scenario: The harbor falls
- **WHEN** the Crown's keep falls
- **THEN** the League wins
