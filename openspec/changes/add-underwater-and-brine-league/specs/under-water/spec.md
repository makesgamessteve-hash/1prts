# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Under the water as a theater: swimming, diving, breath, air, the underwater fog, reefs, pearl beds, wrecks and the diving bell.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Swimming and diving
In deep water the commander SHALL swim: crouch dives, jump rises to the surface, and walking glides; under the surface the fog SHALL close in blue-green.

#### Scenario: Diving to the reef
- **WHEN** the commander wades out into deep water and holds crouch
- **THEN** they sink towards the seabed while the fog closes in blue-green, and rise again while holding jump

### Requirement: Breath
Under the water the commander's breath SHALL run down, and when it is gone they SHALL be hurt; it SHALL refill at the surface or in air, and a breath charm SHALL make it last four times as long.

#### Scenario: Out of breath
- **WHEN** the commander stays under the water past their breath
- **THEN** they start to choke and lose health, and at the surface their breath comes back

### Requirement: Sunken wrecks
A sunken wreck SHALL hold trapped air and a relic that gives the side that finds it aether.

#### Scenario: The sky-barge's relic
- **WHEN** the commander dives to the sunken sky-barge, catches their breath in its hold and opens the relic chest
- **THEN** their breath refills and the side gains aether once

### Requirement: The diving bell
The diving bell SHALL carry whoever stands in it down to the seabed and back, full of air, with a small shell table as a command post.

#### Scenario: Down in the bell
- **WHEN** the commander steps into the diving bell and pulls its lever
- **THEN** the bell goes down to the seabed with them inside, they breathe, and its table shows the war
