# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The *Harbor Siege* scenario (story act IV, the Saltcoast): breaking a Wildborn siege of a Crown
harbor town by sea and by sky.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Harbor Siege
The Saltcoast scenario SHALL start the commander in the war galleon's chart room with a relief fleet at the bay's mouth, SHALL be won when the Wildborn siege camp's war lodge falls, and SHALL be lost if the harbor town's keep falls or the galleon sinks.

#### Scenario: Breaking the siege
- **WHEN** the relief lands its companies and they take the siege camp
- **THEN** the war lodge falls and the scenario is won

#### Scenario: The galleon lost
- **WHEN** the war galleon sinks
- **THEN** the scenario is lost
