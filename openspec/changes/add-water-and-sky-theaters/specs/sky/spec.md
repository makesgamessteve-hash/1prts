# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The sky as a theater: the observation balloon, wyvern riders and the dwarven skyship.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The observation balloon
The Crown's observation balloon SHALL rise on its winch with the commander in its basket and, while up, SHALL see far across the map for its side.

#### Scenario: Up in the basket
- **WHEN** the commander stands in the balloon's basket and works the winch
- **THEN** the basket rises to its full height with the commander, and the side's picture gains sightings far away

### Requirement: Wyvern riders
Wyvern riders SHALL fly over land and water, hunt flyers and units from above, and only missile weapons and towers SHALL reach them.

#### Scenario: Wyverns against a balloon
- **WHEN** wyvern riders are ordered at a raised balloon guarded by men-at-arms and crossbowmen
- **THEN** the men-at-arms cannot strike them, the crossbowmen can, and the wyverns damage the balloon

### Requirement: The skyship
The dwarven skyship SHALL fly to where it is ordered at height, carrying whoever stands on its deck.

#### Scenario: Over the hills by skyship
- **WHEN** the commander stands on the skyship's deck and it is ordered over the hills
- **THEN** it flies there at height with the commander aboard
