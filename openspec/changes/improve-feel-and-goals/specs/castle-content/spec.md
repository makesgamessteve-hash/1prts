# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Harrowmark's ways in and up are all usable by a standing commander.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Headroom on the ways up
Every stair, ramp and passage that a map offers as a way up SHALL leave a standing commander room to walk it, without crouching.

#### Scenario: The keep's stairs to the war room
- **WHEN** the commander walks up the keep's switchback stairs from the great hall
- **THEN** they reach the war room standing up
