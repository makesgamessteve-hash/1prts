# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Comfort while riding and flying in VR.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The vignette when carried
In VR the comfort vignette SHALL also darken the view's edges while the commander is carried by something moving, unless the player turns that off.

#### Scenario: Riding a ship in VR
- **WHEN** the commander stands still on a moving ship in VR
- **THEN** the vignette darkens the edges at the riding strength, and with the riding vignette turned off it stays clear
