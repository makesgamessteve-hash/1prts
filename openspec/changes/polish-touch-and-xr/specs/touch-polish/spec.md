# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Touch controls for every hand and every situation.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Larger buttons, either hand
The touch buttons SHALL be scalable and SHALL be mirrored for left-handed players, with the stick moving to the other half.

#### Scenario: Left-handed with large buttons
- **WHEN** the player sets the touch buttons to 1.5 times their size and left-handed
- **THEN** every button is half again as large, the buttons sit on the left, and the movement stick takes the right half

### Requirement: Buttons that say what they do
Touch buttons SHALL name what they do in the situation: rising and diving in the water, dismounting on a horse.

#### Scenario: Buttons under the water
- **WHEN** the commander swims in deep water on a touch screen
- **THEN** the jump button reads Rise and the crouch button reads Dive
